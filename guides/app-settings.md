# App Settings (Preferences)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this knowledge base](https://help.mywayroute.com/v2/).
{% endhint %}

To open the app settings, open the menu (☰, top right) and tap **Preferences**. Settings of a single route (vehicle, departure time, road restrictions, side of road, optimization mode) aren't here: they're in [Route Settings](how-to-use-myway-route-planner.md#route-settings), below the stop list.

## Proof of Delivery

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Proof of Delivery** | Mark each stop as **Success** or **Failed** and add proof: photos, a signature or details. When it's off, each stop has a single **Done** button. MyWay offers to turn it on the first time you complete a stop. [More](proof-of-delivery.md) | Off |
| **Failure Reasons** | The list of reasons you choose from when a stop fails. Add, edit or delete reasons; **Reset to Defaults** restores the standard list. | Address not found, Customer unavailable, Parcel is wrong |

## Live Activity (iPhone)

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Show Live Activity** | Shows the current stop on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while you're on a route: its address, arrival time and buttons to complete it. [More](understand-navigation-settings.md#live-activity) | On |
| **Auto-Navigate to Next Stop** | When you complete a stop from the Live Activity, directions to the next one open in your navigation app. | On |

## Navigation

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Distance Unit** | Miles (**Mi**) or kilometers (**Km**) for all distances in the app. | Based on your iPhone's region |
| **Navigation App** | The app that opens when you tap **Go**: Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, HERE WeGo, Organic Maps, MAPS.ME, Yandex Maps or Sygic. [More](understand-navigation-settings.md#navigation-apps) | Apple Maps |
| **Navigation Precision** | Passes stops to the navigation app **By GPS Coordinates** or **By Address**. [More](understand-navigation-settings.md#navigation-types-coordinates-or-address) | By GPS Coordinates |

## Geocoder

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Geocoder** | The service that finds addresses when you search: Apple, ArcGIS or HERE. Different providers work better in different regions. [More](../i-cant-find-an-address.md) | Based on your iPhone's region |

## Directions

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Snap to Road** | Moves each stop to the nearest point on a main road, so the route and navigation don't lead you into backyards. The stop may then be a bit farther from the actual location. | On |
| **Allow U-turns on Waypoints** | Lets the route make U-turns at stops. Turning it off can make the route longer. [More](../faq/the-route-myway-produced-doesnt-look-correct.md#settings-that-affect-route-optimization) | On |

## Storage

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Cache** | Shows how much space map data, route previews, downloaded photos, address search results and temporary files take. **Clear Cache** deletes them, and MyWay downloads them again when needed. Your routes and saved places aren't affected. If you updated from the previous version of MyWay, **Previous Version Files** shows the old app's files, and you can clear them too. |
| **Route Files** | Proof of delivery photos and other files of your routes. Clearing the cache doesn't delete them. |

## Other settings and where to find them

* **Default Route Settings and default start and end locations**: the first button under Route Settings while you plan a route. [More](how-to-use-myway-route-planner.md#default-route-settings)
* **Map type, traffic, saved places, ETA labels and your location on the map**: the layers button on the map. [More](how-to-use-myway-route-planner.md#map-layers)
* **Sections of the stop form**: the gear button in the stop form. [More](understanding-stop-settings.md)
* **App language**: iOS Settings > Apps > MyWay > Language. [More](../what-is-myway-route-planner/what-languages-myway-supports.md)
* **Location, contacts, camera and photos access**: iOS Settings > Apps > MyWay.
