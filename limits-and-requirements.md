# Limits and requirements

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this knowledge base](https://help.mywayroute.com/).
{% endhint %}

## Device

* iPhone and iPad with iOS 18.5 or later.
* Live Activity is available on iPhone only.
* Internet connection: needed to search addresses, build and optimize routes, import files and share routes. Following a route works offline. [More](faq/using-myway-without-an-internet-connection-offline-mode.md)

## Routes and stops

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Stops in a route with Premium | Car and Van: 200; Small Truck and Truck: 150; Bicycle and Pedestrian: 50. Completed and skipped stops count; start and end don't. [More](guides/understand-vehicle-settings.md#stop-limits) |
| Stops you can optimize without Premium | 15 |
| Number of routes | Unlimited |
| Addresses in one "Multi Search" (**List**) | 30 lines at a time |
| Import file | .XLSX or .CSV, up to 5 MB, first sheet only |
| Photos per stop (Proof of Delivery) | 5 |
| Stops passed to a navigation app at once (**Navigate through**) | Up to 10 in Apple Maps and Google Maps; 1 in other apps |
| Shared route link (**Share Route Copy**) | Works for 30 days |
| Default time at stop | 5 minutes, adjustable |

## Subscription

* Plans: Monthly and Annual. Prices depend on your country and are shown in the app before you subscribe.
* The free trial is available once per Apple ID.
* A subscription bought on iPhone works on all your iPhones and iPads with the same Apple ID (tap **Restore Purchases** on the other device). It doesn't work on Android, and an Android subscription doesn't work on iPhone.
* Family Sharing is not supported.

## Languages

English, German, Dutch, French, Italian, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish, Ukrainian, Russian, Polish and Romanian. [More](what-is-myway-route-planner/what-languages-myway-supports.md)

## Address search and navigation

* Address search providers: Apple, ArcGIS, HERE. [More](i-cant-find-an-address.md)
* Navigation apps: Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, HERE WeGo, Organic Maps, MAPS.ME, Yandex Maps, Sygic. [More](guides/understand-navigation-settings.md#navigation-apps)
