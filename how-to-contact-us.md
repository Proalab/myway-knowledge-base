# How to contact us?

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/how-to-contact-us).
{% endhint %}

Want to ask a question, suggest a new feature or report a bug? Choose whatever way suits you:

1. **Chat in the app:** open the menu (☰, top right) and tap **Talk to Us**.
2. **Email from the app:** in the same menu, tap **Contact** (in the About section). MyWay adds the app version and your device model to the email, so we can help you faster.
3. **Feedback after a route:** when you complete a route, MyWay asks "How was your route?". If something went wrong, tap **Had Issues** and then **Send Feedback**.
4. **Chat on our website:** go to [https://mywayroute.com](https://mywayroute.com) and talk to us via the online chat.
5. **Email:** write to support@mywayroute.com.

We usually respond faster in online chats, but feel free to use any method that works for you. We will be happy to talk.
