# How many addresses can I add to a route?

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/how-many-addresses-can-i-add-to-a-route).
{% endhint %}

The number of stops in a route depends on your [vehicle type](guides/understand-vehicle-settings.md#stop-limits):

* Car and Van: up to 200 stops
* Small Truck and Truck: up to 150 stops
* Bicycle and Pedestrian: up to 50 stops

Completed and skipped stops count; the start and end locations don't. Of course, you can create as many routes as you like.

Without a Premium subscription, you can optimize routes with [up to 15 stops](what-is-myway-route-planner/how-much-does-myway-route-planner-cost.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
If your route has more stops than your vehicle allows, MyWay shows "Too many stops" when you build the route. Split the route into two or choose another vehicle type.
{% endhint %}

A business version that will allow optimization for up to 500 stops, multi-driver optimization, dispatch functionality, and business subscriptions is coming soon.

To add many stops at once, use the [multi-line import](guides/import-from-file-and-multi-line-import.md#multiline-import) (up to 30 addresses at a time) or [import from a file](guides/import-from-file-and-multi-line-import.md#import-from-file).
