# Glossary

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this knowledge base](https://help.mywayroute.com/v2/).
{% endhint %}

The words MyWay uses in the app, and the other words people often use for the same things.

| Term in MyWay | Also called | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stop** | address, waypoint, location, drop, job, visit, delivery point | A place you need to visit in a route. [More](guides/understanding-stop-settings.md) |
| **Start Location** | depot, origin, starting point, home | Where the route begins. Required. |
| **End Location** | finish, destination, end point, return to depot | Where the route ends. Optional. **Return to Start** makes it the same as the start. |
| **Route** | trip, run, tour, list of stops | A start location, stops and an optional end location. [More](guides/managing-your-routes.md) |
| **Optimize button** | round blue button, arrows button, reroute button, build route | Round blue button with two circular arrows at the bottom right, over the stop list. Builds and optimizes a route you're planning; on a route in progress, appears only when the route is out of date. [More](guides/understand-optimisation-settings.md#where-to-optimize-a-route) |
| **Optimize Stop Order** | optimization, route optimization, best order, sequence | MyWay reorders the stops to build the most efficient route. [More](guides/understand-optimisation-settings.md) |
| **Keep Stop Order** ("Current") | my order, manual order, no optimization | Builds the route in the order you entered the stops. |
| **Update Route** | refresh, recalculate, actualize ETAs | Recalculates the route and ETAs and keeps the stop order. [More](guides/understand-optimisation-settings.md#route-update-and-reoptimize) |
| **Reoptimize Route** | re-optimize, rebuild | Reorders all remaining stops of a route in progress. In the **…** menu of a route in progress only. [More](guides/understand-optimisation-settings.md#where-to-optimize-a-route) |
| **Time at Stop** | service time, stop duration, dwell time, time on site | How long you stay at a stop. [More](faq/what-service-time-should-you-choose.md) |
| **Time Window** | delivery window, appointment time, from–to, open hours | When you need to arrive at a stop. [More](guides/understanding-stop-settings.md#time-windows) |
| **Priority** | ASAP, urgent, first stop, last stop | First, High, Normal or Last. [More](guides/understanding-stop-settings.md#priority-asap-button) |
| **Pickup and Delivery** | linked stops, pick up and drop off, collection | A pickup stop linked to the stops where you deliver its goods. [More](guides/understanding-stop-settings.md#pickup-and-delivery) |
| **Tracking ID** | order number, parcel number, reference, ID | Your number for a stop. **AUTO** numbers all stops. [More](guides/understanding-stop-settings.md#tracking-id) |
| **Proof of Delivery** | POD, visit confirmation, proof of visit, delivery status, delivery confirmation, e-signature | Statuses (Arrived, Success, Failed) and proof (photos, signature, details) for each stop. [More](guides/proof-of-delivery.md) |
| **Skip** | skipped stop, remove from route | Takes a stop out of the route without deleting it; the stop moves to "Skipped". |
| **Road Restrictions** | avoid, avoid tolls, avoid highways | Roads to avoid. [More](guides/understand-route-settings.md#road-restrictions) |
| **Side of Road** | road side, curbside, same side | Which side of the road you stop on. [More](guides/understand-route-settings.md) |
| **Vehicle** | vehicle type, truck mode, bike mode | Car, Van, Small Truck, Truck, Bicycle or Pedestrian. [More](guides/understand-vehicle-settings.md) |
| **Geocoder** | address provider, data provider, geocoding provider, map provider | The service that finds addresses. [More](i-cant-find-an-address.md) |
| **Navigation App** | navigator, GPS app, maps app | The app that gives you turn-by-turn directions. [More](guides/understand-navigation-settings.md) |
| **Live Activity** | Live Widget, lock screen widget, Dynamic Island | The current stop on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. [More](guides/understand-navigation-settings.md#live-activity) |
| **Saved Places** | places, favorites, favorite places, frequent addresses | Addresses you save because you visit them often. [More](guides/managing-your-contacts-and-places.md#places) |
| **Share Route Copy** | share link, send route | A link to a copy of your route. [More](guides/export-your-route-and-share-it-with-others.md#share-route-copy) |
| **Report** / **Export & Share** | export, download, PDF, Excel, spreadsheet | Route files: XLS, CSV, PDF and the manifest. [More](guides/export-your-route-and-share-it-with-others.md) |
| **Premium** | subscription, paid plan, pro, full version | The paid plan: Monthly or Annual. [More](what-is-myway-route-planner/how-much-does-myway-route-planner-cost.md) |
| **ETA** | arrival time, estimated time | When you're expected to arrive at a stop. "\~" means approximate. |
