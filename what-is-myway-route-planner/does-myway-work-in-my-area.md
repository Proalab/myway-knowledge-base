# Does MyWay work in my area?

Yes. MyWay combines the most up-to-date maps, addresses and places databases from Google Maps, Mapbox, HERE, TomTom, Apple Maps, ArcGIS and OpenStreetMap into one route planner. That's why MyWay works in every region of the planet and has the most extensive database of addresses across the globe.

We work in most countries, and most of our users work and live in:

* USA
* UK
* Canada
* Australia
* New Zealand
* Brazil
* Germany
* France
* Italy
* Netherlands
* Europe
* APAC
* all other countries around the globe: we serve customers in [175 countries](https://www.mywayroute.com/about)

Wherever you are, we can help you build efficient routes.

{% hint style="success" %}
**A feature no other route planner has:** you can [change the address geocoding provider](../i-cant-find-an-address.md) to Apple, ArcGIS or HERE. By default, MyWay picks the provider that works best for your iPhone's region. If you know that another provider works better in your area, open the menu (☰, top right) and tap **Preferences** > **Geocoder**.
{% endhint %}
