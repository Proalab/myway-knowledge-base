# What languages MyWay supports?

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/what-is-myway-route-planner/what-languages-myway-supports).
{% endhint %}

### MyWay Route Planner is translated into

* English
* German
* Dutch
* French
* Italian
* Spanish (Spain and Latin America)
* Portuguese (Brazil)
* Turkish
* Ukrainian
* Russian
* Polish
* Romanian

We are also working on official translations into:

* Arabic
* Hebrew
* Hindi
* Indonesian
* Malay
* Thai

### How to change the app language?

MyWay uses your phone's language. If MyWay isn't available in that language, it uses English.

To use MyWay in a different language than your phone:

1. Open iOS **Settings** > **Apps** > **MyWay**.
2. Tap **Language** and choose the language.

### Translation issues

Found a mistake in the translation or have a better wording in mind? Tell us: open the menu (☰, top right) and tap **Talk to Us**, or email us at support@mywayroute.com.
