# What's new in MyWay 3

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** The Android app is still on version 2. See [Version 2 of this knowledge base](https://help.mywayroute.com/v2/).
{% endhint %}

MyWay 3 is the new version of MyWay Route Planner for iPhone and iPad, with a new design, new features and many settings in new places. Here's what happens to your data after the update and where to find things now.

## Your data after the update

The first time you open MyWay 3, you see "Welcome to the all-new MyWay 3!", and MyWay moves your data from the previous version: your routes (including the active one) and your saved places. It happens on your iPhone automatically; you don't need to do anything.

If MyWay couldn't move something, you see "Couldn't move everything", and MyWay tries again the next time you open the app. Nothing is lost.

A few things change during the move:

* Stops with ASAP priority get High priority.
* Proof of delivery photos and signatures move with your routes. Video and document proofs aren't supported in MyWay 3 and aren't moved.
* Links to routes shared from the previous version still open, but without proof of delivery.

## Where to find things now

* **Menu** (☰, top right): **Routes**, **Places**, **Preferences**, **Talk to Us**, **Help** and your subscription.
* **Route Settings** (vehicle, departure time, road restrictions, side of road, optimization mode): below the stop list while you plan a route. [More](../guides/how-to-use-myway-route-planner.md#route-settings)
* **Navigation options**: tap **…** next to **Go** to open "Navigation to Stop". [More](../guides/understand-navigation-settings.md)
* **Update or reoptimize a route in progress**: tap **…** in the route header > **Reoptimize Route**. [More](../guides/understand-optimisation-settings.md#route-update-and-reoptimize)
* **Default start and end locations**: **Route Settings** > **Start & End Locations**. [More](../guides/understanding-stop-settings.md#start-and-finish-settings)

## New in MyWay 3

* **Navigate through several stops** in Apple Maps or Google Maps in one tap, plus new navigation apps, including Sygic. [More](../guides/understand-navigation-settings.md#navigate-through-several-stops)
* **Live Activity**: the current stop on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with buttons to complete it. [More](../guides/understand-navigation-settings.md#live-activity)
* **Pickup and delivery**: link the stop where you pick up goods to the stops where you deliver them. [More](../guides/understanding-stop-settings.md#pickup-and-delivery)
* **Proof of delivery**: up to 5 photos per stop and your own failure reasons. [More](../guides/proof-of-delivery.md)
* **Stop priorities**: First, High, Normal and Last. [More](../guides/understanding-stop-settings.md#priority-asap-button)
* **Avoid unpaved roads** in Road Restrictions. [More](../guides/how-to-use-myway-route-planner.md#road-restrictions)
* **Contacts** with company, website and messengers, and more stop details: premises, package count, tracking ID. [More](../guides/understanding-stop-settings.md)
* **Import from a web link** to an .XLSX or .CSV file. [More](../guides/import-from-file-and-multi-line-import.md#import-from-file)
* **New Liquid Glass design.**

If something works differently than you expect, tell us: open the menu and tap **Talk to Us**.
