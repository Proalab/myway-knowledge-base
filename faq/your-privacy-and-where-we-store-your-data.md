# Your privacy and where we store your data

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/faq/your-privacy-and-where-we-store-your-data).
{% endhint %}

We, as the MyWay Route Planner team, care about your privacy and believe all data belongs to you and should not be accessed by anyone except you.

## Where are my routes, stops and proof of delivery stored?

Your routes, stops, saved places and proof of delivery (photos, signatures, details) are stored on your iPhone and can't be accessed by other apps. We do not store your routes on our servers. When a feature needs our servers (like optimizing a route, importing a file or sharing your route with others), this data is not linked to you personally and can't be used to restore your routes.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Because your data is stored on your iPhone, deleting the app deletes your routes and saved places. MyWay doesn't sync them through iCloud. Before you delete the app or move to a new phone, save the routes you need, for example as a PDF report or a [route copy](../guides/export-your-route-and-share-it-with-others.md#share-route-copy).
{% endhint %}

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own marketing. To learn what data we collect and how we process it, please read our [Privacy Policy](https://www.mywayroute.com/privacy-policy).

## Sensitive Data

When you share your route with others, you might unintentionally share data like phone numbers, addresses and customer names with the people you share your routes with. Please share only the absolute required minimum and clean the data before sharing your routes with others if possible. Your customers' data safety should be your primary concern when you share it with others.
