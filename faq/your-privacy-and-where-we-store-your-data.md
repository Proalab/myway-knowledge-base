# Your privacy and where we store your data

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/v2/faq/your-privacy-and-where-we-store-your-data).
{% endhint %}

We, the MyWay Route Planner team, care about your privacy. We believe all your data belongs to you and shouldn't be accessed by anyone except you.

## Where are my routes, stops and proof of delivery stored?

Your routes, stops, saved places and proof of delivery (photos, signatures, details) are stored on your iPhone, and other apps can't access them. We do not store your routes on our servers. When a feature needs our servers (like optimizing a route, importing a file or sharing your route with others), the data is not linked to you personally and can't be used to restore your routes.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Because your data is stored on your iPhone, deleting the app deletes your routes and saved places. MyWay doesn't sync them through iCloud. Before you delete the app or move to a new phone, save the routes you need, for example as a PDF report or a [route copy](../guides/export-your-route-and-share-it-with-others.md#share-route-copy).
{% endhint %}

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own marketing. To learn what data we collect and how we process it, read our [Privacy Policy](https://www.mywayroute.com/privacy-policy).

## Sensitive Data

When you share a route, you may also share phone numbers, addresses and customer names with the people you send it to. Share only what's really needed, and remove extra data before sharing if you can. Your customers' data safety should be your first concern when you share it with others.
