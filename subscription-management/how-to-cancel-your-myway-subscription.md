# How to cancel your MyWay subscription?

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/v2/subscription-management/how-to-cancel-your-myway-subscription).
{% endhint %}

## **During your trial** <a href="#during-your-trial" id="during-your-trial"></a>

There are no charges during your trial. If you keep using the app after the trial ends, you're charged automatically. To avoid the charge, cancel your subscription at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

{% hint style="warning" %}
We do not store your credit card details and do not charge your bank account directly. All purchases on iPhone or iPad go through the Apple App Store, and all purchases on Android devices are handled by Google Play.
{% endhint %}

To cancel, follow the steps for your device.

### **Apple App Store (iPhone, iPad)** <a href="#h_1bf3e7cb20" id="h_1bf3e7cb20"></a>

In MyWay:

1. Open the menu (☰, top right).
2. In the **Subscription** section, tap **Manage Subscription**.
3. Tap **Cancel Subscription**.
4. Confirm the cancellation.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you don't see **Manage Subscription** in the menu, MyWay doesn't see an active subscription. Try [**Restore Purchases**](myway-does-not-recognize-my-subscription.md) first, or cancel in iPhone Settings.
{% endhint %}

In iPhone Settings:

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Make sure you're signed in with the correct Apple ID.
3. Tap your name, then **Subscriptions**.
4. Tap the MyWay subscription.
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription**.

More details are in the [Apple Support article](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).

### Google Play Store (Android) <a href="#h_dabe3cfaa2" id="h_dabe3cfaa2"></a>

Using a direct link:

1. Make sure you're signed in with the Google account you used to subscribe.
2. Open [this link](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions) on your Android device.
3. Select the MyWay subscription.
4. Tap **Cancel Subscription**.

In the Google Play app:

1. Open the Google Play app.
2. Tap the profile icon (top right).
3. Tap **Payments & subscriptions** > **Subscriptions**.
4. Select the MyWay subscription.
5. Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the instructions.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If MyWay is not in your list of subscriptions (in iPhone Settings or Google Play), you don't have an active subscription for this account, and you won't be billed.
{% endhint %}

## **After canceling** <a href="#after-cancelling" id="after-cancelling"></a>

You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. The menu shows when it expires ("Expires: …").

If you're canceling because of issues with MyWay, it'd be great if you could send us a quick message: tap **Talk to Us** in the app menu. This way, we can keep on improving the app for other drivers. We will appreciate it a lot!
