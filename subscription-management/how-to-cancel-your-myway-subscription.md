# How to cancel your MyWay subscription?

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/subscription-management/how-to-cancel-your-myway-subscription).
{% endhint %}

## **During your trial** <a href="#during-your-trial" id="during-your-trial"></a>

{% hint style="warning" %}
We do not store your credit card details and do not charge your bank account directly. All purchases on iPhone or iPad go through the Apple App Store, and all purchases on Android devices are handled by Google Play.
{% endhint %}

There are no charges during your trial. If you continue using the app after the trial period, you'll be automatically charged. To prevent charges when your trial ends, please cancel your subscription at least 24 hours before the trial ends. You can find the cancellation instructions below:

### **Apple App Store (iPhone, iPad)** <a href="#h_1bf3e7cb20" id="h_1bf3e7cb20"></a>

* Tap the menu button (three lines, top right corner of the map)
* In the "Subscription" section, tap "Manage Subscription" (\*)
* Tap "Cancel Subscription"
* Confirm the cancellation

Alternatively, you can cancel your subscription from your iPhone Settings

* Open the Settings app
* Make sure you're signed in with the correct Apple ID
* Tap your name
* Tap "Subscriptions"
* Select the MyWay subscription (\*\*)
* Tap "Cancel Subscription"

More details are in the [Apple Support article](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).

### Google Play Store (Android) <a href="#h_dabe3cfaa2" id="h_dabe3cfaa2"></a>

* Make sure you're signed in with the correct email address
* Open [this link](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions) on your Android device
* Select the MyWay subscription (\*\*)
* Tap "Cancel Subscription"

Alternatively, you can cancel your subscription from the Google Play App

* Open the Google Play app.
* At the top right, tap the profile icon.
* Tap Payments & subscriptions ⇾ Subscriptions.
* Select the MyWay subscription (\*\*).
* Tap "Cancel subscription"
* Follow the instructions.

{% hint style="warning" %}
\*If "Manage Subscription" is not shown in the menu, MyWay doesn't see an active subscription. Try ["Restore Purchases"](myway-does-not-recognize-my-subscription.md) first, or cancel in iPhone Settings.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
\*\*If MyWay is not shown in your list of subscriptions, it means you don't have an active subscription for this account, and you won't be billed.
{% endhint %}

## **After canceling** <a href="#after-cancelling" id="after-cancelling"></a>

After canceling, you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. The menu shows the date when it expires ("Expires: …").

If you're canceling due to issues with MyWay, it'd be great if you could send us a quick message: tap "Talk to Us" in the app menu. This way, we can keep on improving the app for other drivers. We will appreciate it a lot!
