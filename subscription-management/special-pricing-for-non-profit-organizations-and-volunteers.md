# Special pricing for non-profit organizations and volunteers

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/subscription-management/special-pricing-for-non-profit-organizations-and-volunteers).
{% endhint %}

We love people who are passionate about helping others. If you have a non-profit organization or help others as a volunteer, please contact us: in the app, tap the menu button (three lines, top right corner of the map) and then "Talk to Us", or "Contact" to send us an email. Please share your story with us, and we can offer you a special price.

{% hint style="info" %}
We might ask you for proof, as we have many requests like this. We want to be sure we are not giving discounted access to everybody but only those who deserve it. After all, if we give everyone free access, we can't play our part and help communities ourselves.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
We provide special pricing as a promo code promotion. It must follow App Store rules and is limited by platform abilities, so promotions are not equal. We'll tell you how to activate it.
{% endhint %}
