# Which payment methods are supported?

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/subscription-management/which-payment-methods-are-supported).
{% endhint %}

All purchases on iPhone or iPad go through the Apple App Store and are charged to your Apple ID. Purchases on Android phones and tablets go through the Google Play Store. So the payment methods you can use are the ones your store accepts.

### **Apple App Store (iPhone, iPad)** <a href="#ios-iphone-or-ipad" id="ios-iphone-or-ipad"></a>

See Apple's list of supported payment methods and information about [Apple Pay here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201469).

To change your payment method, see [our article](how-to-change-or-add-a-payment-method.md) or [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118293).

### Google Play Store (Android) <a href="#h_596faec68a" id="h_596faec68a"></a>

See Google's list of supported payment methods on [Google Pay here](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2651410?co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DUnited+States\&hl=en). Make sure to switch the page to your region.

To change your payment method, see [Google Support](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/4646404?hl=en\&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid).
