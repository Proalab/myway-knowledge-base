# Which payment methods are supported?

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/subscription-management/which-payment-methods-are-supported).
{% endhint %}

All purchases on iPhone and iPad go through the Apple App Store, and payment is charged to your Apple ID account.

### **Apple App Store (iPhone, iPad)** <a href="#ios-iphone-or-ipad" id="ios-iphone-or-ipad"></a>

You can find more information about supported payment methods and information about [Apple Pay here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201469).

To change your payment method on the Apple App Store, follow the detailed instructions in this article: [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118293) or [our article](how-to-change-or-add-a-payment-method.md).
