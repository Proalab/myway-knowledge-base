# I've been double charged

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/subscription-management/ive-been-double-charged).
{% endhint %}

First of all, a double charge is not a common issue, but it might still happen because of the nature of the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. The good news is it is pretty easy to sort out.

There are a few reasons you might see a double charge from MyWay:

#### 1. Hold not correctly handled by your bank <a href="#h_f37520469e" id="h_f37520469e"></a>

If you've recently subscribed to MyWay, a double charge might be caused by a hold. Apple places a hold on your account to check that there are sufficient funds. As soon as your free trial ends and the first charge goes through, Apple removes the hold. Unfortunately, some banks don't handle this correctly: they show the hold as a charge and may take up to a month to show that it has been removed. Please let us know if the hold isn't removed after 1 month.

#### 2. Your previous payment was late <a href="#h_845ae006ea" id="h_845ae006ea"></a>

If you paid your subscription fee late last month, Apple could charge you twice: for last month and for this month. A double charge can also happen if Apple couldn't charge your payment method, your subscription was canceled automatically, and you've now restarted it.

#### 3. Subscribed with two Apple IDs <a href="#h_fa2e6e75db" id="h_fa2e6e75db"></a>

One Apple ID can't have two active MyWay subscriptions at the same time. If you've been charged twice, you might have subscribed with two different Apple IDs, for example on two devices. You can check [your Apple purchases here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204088). [Cancel](how-to-cancel-your-myway-subscription.md) the one you don't need and [apply for a refund](how-can-i-get-a-refund.md).

If you've accidentally bought a subscription twice or started 2 free trials on Google Play, [you can check that here](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Cancel one of them and apply for a refund (see [Version 2 of this knowledge base](https://help.mywayroute.com/subscription-management/how-can-i-get-a-refund) for Google Play).

If you switched from the Monthly to the Annual plan in MyWay, you are not charged twice: the annual plan starts at the next renewal date of your monthly plan.

#### 4. Started a subscription on both iPhone and Android <a href="#h_de3ce1f52b" id="h_de3ce1f52b"></a>

You might have started a subscription on both an Apple and an Android device. You can [check that here for Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204088) and [here for Android](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Cancel the subscription you don't want to use anymore and apply for a refund: on iPhone, see [how to cancel](how-to-cancel-your-myway-subscription.md) and [how to get a refund](how-can-i-get-a-refund.md); on Android, see [Version 2 of this knowledge base](https://help.mywayroute.com/subscription-management/how-to-cancel-your-myway-subscription).

#### 5. Other <a href="#h_b60472697a" id="h_b60472697a"></a>

If none of the above helps, you can dispute all charges at your bank. If you wait for 1 month, all disputed charges will be canceled, and you can safely start a new subscription. If that doesn't help either, please [contact us](../how-to-contact-us.md) and send us a screenshot of all charges, and we will be able to assist you.
