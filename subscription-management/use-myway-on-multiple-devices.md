# Use MyWay on multiple devices

{% hint style="info" %}
**Using MyWay on Android?** This page describes the iOS app version 3. See [Version 2 of this page](https://help.mywayroute.com/v2/subscription-management/use-myway-on-multiple-devices).
{% endhint %}

You can use your MyWay subscription on several iPhones and iPads. To set up another device:

1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.
2. Install MyWay.
3. [Restore the subscription](myway-does-not-recognize-my-subscription.md): open the menu (☰, top right) and tap **Restore Purchases**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Your routes and saved places are stored on each device separately and don't sync between devices. To move a route to another device, [share a route copy](../guides/export-your-route-and-share-it-with-others.md#share-route-copy) and open the link on that device.
{% endhint %}

Keep in mind that **you can't share a subscription with another driver**: each driver needs their own.

A subscription bought on iPhone doesn't work on Android, and the other way round. On Android, sign in with the Google account you used to subscribe and check the subscription as described in [Version 2 of this knowledge base](https://help.mywayroute.com/v2/subscription-management/myway-does-not-recognize-my-subscription).

To [change your payment method](how-to-change-or-add-a-payment-method.md) or [cancel the subscription](how-to-cancel-your-myway-subscription.md) later, use the platform you originally subscribed on (iOS or Android).
