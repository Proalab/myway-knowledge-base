# Does app support Family Sharing on iOS?

Family Sharing lets you and the other members of your household share App Store purchases and subscriptions, so you don't have to buy them several times. Unfortunately, at this point, MyWay does not support Family Sharing, so you can't share one subscription with your family members. The main reason is that route history and other settings that affect route optimization can't be shared.

We are sorry about that and will work to sort this out in the future. For now, the only option is a separate subscription for each member of your family.

{% hint style="info" %}
If your family uses Ask to Buy, MyWay shows "Purchase is awaiting approval" until the family organizer approves the purchase.
{% endhint %}
